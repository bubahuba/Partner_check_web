# PartnerCheck - Privacy & Contact Access Explanation

## Why We Need Contact Access

PartnerCheck requests **read-only access** to your contacts for one purpose: to let you **manually select** contacts through your device's contact picker.

**Important:** We do NOT automatically access all your contacts. You choose which contacts to add.

## What We Do With Your Data

### When You Select a Contact:
1. We read the **name** and **phone number** of the contact you select
2. We convert the phone number to a color code (using the last 6 digits)
3. We store **only** the name and color value locally on your device
4. **We do NOT store the phone number itself**

### What We Store:
- ✅ Contact name
- ✅ Color value (derived from phone number)
- ❌ Phone number (discarded after color calculation)
- ❌ Email addresses
- ❌ Any other contact information

## Your Privacy

- **All data stays on your device** - nothing is sent to servers
- **No third-party sharing** - your data is never shared
- **You control everything** - choose which contacts to add, delete anytime
- **Read-only access** - we never modify your contacts

## You Can Use the App Without Permission

You can add contacts manually by entering the name and phone number yourself - no contact permission needed.

---

**Bottom Line:** We only access contacts you explicitly select, store minimal data locally, and never share anything with anyone.
